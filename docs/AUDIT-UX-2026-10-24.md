## Click path — landing to monetize start

**Before (prod):** Hero “Start your 14-day free trial” (1) → fill company, name, email, phone → “Go to step 2” (2) → card. A second path, PPV “Start streaming,” landed on `/auth` with a different promise (“no card”). Nav trial and “Pricing” were the same destination.

**After:** “Start selling,” the hero trial button, nav “Start free trial,” homepage PPV “Start selling,” `/ppv`, or `/auth?mode=signup` (1) → the same modal, email only → “Go to step 2” (2) → card on file, $0 today. One sentence (`TRIAL_CARD_PROMISE`) on the hero, the modal, pricing-adjacent CTAs, and creator signup. Pricing in the nav scrolls to plans. Fan signup (`as=member`) stays free and cardless. “Create a login instead” on `/auth` is a login, and the copy says it does not take a card.

Fields on step 1: 4 → 1. Clicks to the card step: still 2. Paths that used to disagree (trial modal vs “no card” account wall): 2 → 1.

## What changed

- `HomepageHero` — income headline, “Start selling,” Connect Stripe → Set price → Go live, one fee line.
- `HomepageNav` — Discover, Artist sites; trial button opens the modal.
- `TrialSignupModal` — step 1 is email only. Name for Stripe metadata is the email local-part when none was collected.
- `Auth` — creator `/auth?mode=signup` opens the same trial modal. Fan (`as=member`) signup stays free and cardless. While the modal is open, Auth does not redirect the new session away from the card step.
- `useInstallPrompt` / `FoundersStickyCTA` — not on the first viewport. The FIRST 100 banner is not in this tree’s landing (it was already absent versus production). No unlabeled floating control is mounted on the logged-out homepage; the install card is labeled and waits for scroll.
- Pay-per-view CTAs (`/` PPV block and `/ppv`) open the same trial modal. The “free to start” line on `/ppv` is the shared card sentence.

## Classroom

Community spaces keep every existing tab. Community, Classroom, and Members sit on the bar; Calendar, Events, Store, Leaderboard, Pricing, Earn, Blasts, and About sit under More. The feed opens with a compose button. Owner reports, blasts, and the post-level control stay inside “Manage community.” An empty classroom asks the owner to add the first module, then the lesson.

## Round 2

- Waitlist and the post-application screen say what happens next: get approved, then membership / tickets / pay-per-view, then go live. The approval gate stays.
- Dashboard wizard is titled “Create your first paid offer” and lists membership, tickets, pay-per-view, and tips first. A shortcut row stays on the dashboard after the wizard is dismissed. Membership opens community pricing. Pay-per-view and tips open go-live.
- Community posts show more of the body, open from the keyboard, and the like control is named. Compose stays pinned under the tab bar.
- Lesson edit and delete buttons name the lesson. The public hero drops the forced full-viewport height on small screens so the two CTAs and the 3-step strip sit closer together.
